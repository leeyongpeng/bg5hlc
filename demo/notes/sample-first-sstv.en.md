I had only seen SSTV in videos before. Receiving a frame myself felt completely
different.

## How it was done

1. Tune the radio to 14.230 MHz, USB
2. Audio cable into the computer's sound card
3. MMSSTV in Auto mode, wait for the sync header

The first frame missed, the second came through. There is a slight slant on the
right edge because part of the sync header was lost — that is the interesting
part of SSTV: every picture carries the conditions of the moment.

## A few notes

- Do not push the volume too hard; clipping shows up as bright horizontal bars
- 14.230 is the usual 20m frequency, easiest on weekend mornings
- Log the contact and send a card back
