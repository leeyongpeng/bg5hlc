title: A weekend activation by the river
summary: Took the FT-818 and a 20m dipole to the riverbank and worked 14 stations in one morning.
tags: [POTA, Portable]
---

Left at 6:30 and reached the river by 7:15. The water still had mist on it, and
the birds were loud enough to hear while I was putting the antenna up.

## Gear

- Yaesu FT-818ND, 5 W
- 20m dipole, centre at about 8 m, ends tied to two willows
- 12 V LiFePO4 pack, enough for a morning

## How it went

20m was dead when I switched on, so I listened on 40m first and worked one
station in Jiangsu. After eight o'clock 20m opened and Japan and Southeast Asia
came in, with Jakarta the farthest.

> The best part of operating in the field is the noise floor. Back home the
> S-meter sits at S5 all day; out here it is almost S0.

Packed up at noon with 14 QSOs, five of them new callsigns. Light rain started
while I was taking the antenna down — just in time to get everything in the car.
